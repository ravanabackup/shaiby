      Every cycle and every person is different — this is built from typical hormonal patterns, not from her actual biology, and it isn't medical advice. Cramps, mood, and energy can be affected by stress, health conditions, and plenty else this can't see.

      The single best way to make this accurate: ask her when her period actually starts each month and update the date above. If anything feels off — very heavy bleeding, severe pain, or mood swings that feel bigger than a bad week — that's worth a doctor's visit, not just patience.
